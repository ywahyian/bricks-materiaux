Questions

What builders ask us.

Do you have it in stock?

The standard ranges are in stock in Zellik, across more than 8,000 m². Need a specific product? Add it to your request and we confirm availability with the quote.

Where do you deliver?

Mainly across Flemish Brabant, the Brussels-Capital Region and the towns around them. Site a bit further out? Just ask.

My site is hard to reach.

Our trucks unload by crane or pallet jack. Describe the access when you order (narrow street, upper floor, no curb) and we send the right vehicle.

How fast do I get my quote?

Within 24 hours. The more precise your request (products, quantities, date), the more precise the quote.

Can I come to the depot?

Yes. Monday to Friday, 6 am to 5 pm, Saturday by appointment. On-site parking at Nieuwe Steenweg 8, 1731 Zellik.

You’re new. Why should I trust you?

True, we opened in July 2024. We’re a family business that lives on repeat customers. Ask for a first quote and judge for yourself.
